GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is Same Day Loan accepting applications?

No. This website is a non-operational prototype. It does not accept or transmit loan applications, make credit decisions, or provide loans.

What loan amounts are shown?

The concept uses a requested range of $5,000 to $30,000. This is not a statement that these amounts are currently available. Actual limits depend on licensing, state law, underwriting, and approved products.

Does the calculator show my actual payment?

No. It uses an example APR and term selected by you. Actual APR, fees, payment, and total cost must be disclosed by an authorized lender before you agree to a loan.

Can I log in or make a payment?

Not yet. Customer accounts, payment processing, loan servicing, and the lender administration portal have not been built or connected.

When could funds arrive?

Funding speed depends on approval, completed loan documents, payment method, bank processing, and applicable law. This site makes no promise of same-day funding.
